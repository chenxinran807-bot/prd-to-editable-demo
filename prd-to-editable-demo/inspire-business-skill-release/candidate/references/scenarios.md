# Scenario Index

## Image input

Show clear camera and album choices as labeled actions. Explain photo requirements in text. Include permission denial, invalid image, retry and return paths when relevant.

## Product discovery

Keep discovery content dominant. Filters, tabs or recommendations appear only when supported by the PRD. Product cards must not invent commercial facts.

If the PRD provides no catalog fixture, demonstrate information architecture with `商品 A/B/C`, neutral media blocks and `价格待提供`. Omit marketing tags rather than filling the surface with realistic-looking examples.

## Cart and purchase intent

Keep selected state, quantity changes, unavailable items and action consequences understandable. Do not imply a completed payment flow unless requested.

## Personal or generated result

Separate processing, result, failure and regeneration states. Preserve the user's ability to return or retry without losing the accepted prior state.
